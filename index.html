Why Time Paver

Hand-laying is slow, heavy, and it costs you crew.

Every paver bent over, pinched, and set by hand adds up: sore backs, wrecked knees, bruised fingers, a short crew by Friday. The Time Paver puts the load on the vacuum.

Traditional hand-laying

  • Crew on their knees. Hours of kneeling and bending, every day.
  • Pinched fingers and strained joints. Hands take the abuse on every stone.
  • Heavy lifting, one at a time. Fatigue slows the whole crew by afternoon.
  • Slower production. Delays, overtime, and lost labor hours.

With the Time Paver

  • Operators stay on their feet. Vacuum suction lifts and places the stone.
  • Protects fingers and joints. No more pinch points between pavers.
  • Lift a multitude of pavers with ease. Less effort, less fatigue, more stone down.
  • Faster work, lower cost. Save time and money on every job site.
